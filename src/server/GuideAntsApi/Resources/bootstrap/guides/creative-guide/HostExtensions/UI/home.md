## How Creative Guide Works

## Get Started!

- Click ***New Conversation** to kick off a session with Creative Guide.
- Upload files or describe your challenge–Guide will organize your work and deliver downloadable outputs, diagrams, media, code, and more.
- Select assistants manually or let the Guide route your requests automatically.
- Collaborate, share, and track everything in one workspace.
- Change the notebook home page: Right click a file and choose **Set as Notebook Home Page** to see it, instead of this.

Want a different visual, audio snippet, or interactive chart? Just ask–the Creative Guide can revise, expand, or personalize your onboarding!
